## Patterns (what the ≥10k videos share)
1. **Comparison, not lecture.** Tier lists, "know the difference", left vs right, "every level". The viewer judges → comments.
2. **Lists of exercises for one muscle** with the muscle lit up ("5 exercises for…", "12 best…"). Highly saved.
3. **Myth-bust / "I tested"** with a clear yes/no answer.
4. **Faceless animated accounts win when the look is the same every time** (bluegym's blue mascot, workouttips49's red-muscle 3D). People recognise the style before the name.
5. Hooks are 4–8 words, a claim or a contradiction. Captions short + "save this".
6. App brands (Lyfta, Hevy) only get reach when the video is pure value; app demos flop except one.
