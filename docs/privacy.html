Privacy Policy

Forge – AI gym coach · Last updated 2 November 2026

Forge is made by Preslav Stoyanov (the Netherlands), who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: forge.gym.you@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored hashed by our auth provider).
  • Your plan: answers from onboarding – goal, sex, birth year, height, weight, training days, experience, gym type, food notes and coach style.
  • Training: workouts, exercises, sets, weights and reps you log.
  • Food: meals you log (food names, grams, calories and macros).
  • Meal photos: when you use photo scan, the photo is sent once to our AI provider to estimate the food. Forge does not store the photo.
  • Health data (only if you allow it): from Apple Health or Health Connect we read weight, body fat, lean mass, steps, active calories, sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability. Forge only reads this data and never writes to it.

Why we use it

  • To calculate your calorie and protein targets, build your training plan and suggest weights.
  • To show your trends (weight, recovery) and adjust your plan each week.
  • To generate short coach messages and meal estimates with AI.
  • To keep your data in sync between devices and to provide support.

Legal basis (GDPR): performance of our contract with you (providing the app) and, for health data, your explicit consent, which you give in the system permission screen and can withdraw at any time in your phone settings.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not show ads.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase – database, login and server functions (data hosted in the EU).
  • Anthropic – AI model that writes coach messages and estimates meal photos. Only the numbers or photo needed for that request are sent.
  • Apple / Google – app stores and subscription payments. We do not receive your card details.
  • RevenueCat – manages subscription status (an anonymous user ID and purchase status).

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and all data linked to it are deleted from our database immediately. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you sign out, delete the account or uninstall the app.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict processing. Delete your account any time in the app under You → Delete account, or email us. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Age

Forge is for people aged 18 and over.

Not medical advice

Forge gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet or training if you have a health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
