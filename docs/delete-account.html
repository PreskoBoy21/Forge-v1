Delete your Forge account

Forge: AI Gym Coach · developer: Preslav Stoyanov

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Forge and log in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Yes, delete everything.

Your account and all its data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

Can't open the app? Email forge.gym.you@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Forge account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login)
  • Your profile and plan (goal, body stats, training days, food notes, coach style)
  • Workouts, sets and weights you logged
  • Meals and food you logged
  • Health data synced from Health Connect or Apple Health (weight, body fat, sleep, heart rate, steps)
  • Weekly check-ins and calorie targets

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Meal photos are never stored. Subscription purchase records are kept by Google Play / the App Store under their own policies; deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Google Play or App Store settings.

See also our Privacy Policy.
