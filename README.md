# Forge

Forge is a fitness app for people who already go to the gym but still feel a bit lost.

Most apps ask you to track everything. Forge tells you **what to do today**: the workout, how much protein is left, and a simple next meal. At night you close the day, get a score, and your friends can see that you showed up — not your weight, not your calories.

The idea is a small daily habit:

1. Open the app in the morning and get **one plan** (the Daily Call)
2. Train in **Gym Mode** (big buttons, swap if a machine is taken)
3. **Snap** a meal, then quickly fix the estimate
4. **Forge the day** at night — a score for following the plan
5. Check your **Crew** (who trained, who needs a nudge)
6. On Sunday, read the **Weekly Verdict** — keep going, eat a bit more, or take it easier

This project is a working prototype of that experience. You can tap through the real screens with sample data. The full product (accounts, live meal scanning, subscriptions) is designed but not wired up yet.

**Who it’s for:** gym-goers, roughly 18–30, who want a clear plan without becoming a nutrition expert.

## Try it

```bash
npm install
npx expo start
```

Open it on your phone with the Expo Go app, or in a browser. On the first screen, choose **See the daily loop**.
