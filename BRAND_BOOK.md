# FORGE BRAND BOOK v1 (Presko, 2026-11-24) — every agent reads this FIRST
Built from: Influence (Cialdini), Contagious (Berger), This Is Marketing (Godin), Made to Stick (Heath), Everybody Writes (Handley), StoryBrand 2.0 (Miller), Jab Jab Jab Right Hook (Vaynerchuk), They Ask You Answer (Sheridan), Hacking Growth (Ellis/Brown).

## 1. Who we are for (Godin: smallest viable audience)
**"Alex":** 18–30, lifts 0–3 years, wants to lose fat or recomp, eats "clean" but the scale won't move, drowns in conflicting advice. Every video is written to ONE Alex ("you", never "guys").
Identity line: **People like us track, train smart and don't fall for myths.**

## 2. One-liner (StoryBrand)
Most lifters waste months guessing what to eat and how to train. Forge tells you exactly what to do today — so you finally see progress.
- The VIEWER is the hero. Forge is the guide (empathy + authority). Forge never brags.
- Every video = problem Alex has → stakes ("you'll stay stuck") → simple fix → success picture.

## 3. Voice (Handley)
- Short words, 5–10-word sentences, one idea per sentence. Cut every word that doesn't earn its place.
- Direct, warm, a bit cheeky. Never preachy, never shaming bodies, never "bro science".
- Say: "Your snack is just tiny." Never: "You have no discipline."
- Numbers beat adjectives: "300 kcal", "150 g chicken", never "healthy", "a lot".
- Every caption must be useful OR empathetic, ideally both.

## 4. The script checklist — score before rendering (Made to Stick SUCCESs + Contagious STEPPS)
Score each 0–2. **Render only if total ≥ 14/20; otherwise rewrite.** Write the score into script.md.
| Check | Question |
|---|---|
| Simple | ONE idea a 12-year-old can repeat? |
| Unexpected | Does second 0–2 break a belief or show a surprise? |
| Concrete | Real grams/kcal/lifts/foods on screen? |
| Credible | Number is true (USDA/study) and "see for yourself" visual? |
| Emotional | Is it about Alex's own body/day/frustration? |
| Story | Is there a recognisable moment ("4 pm hunger", "Monday chest day")? |
| Social currency | Would sharing it make Alex look smart? |
| Trigger | Tied to a daily moment (weekday, meal, gym time)? |
| Practical value | Worth saving? |
| Public | Brand kit visible (series chip, F, end card, sound logo)? |

## 5. Persuasion rules (Cialdini) — honest only
- Reciprocity: free, genuinely useful value every post. Audience mode = 100% give.
- Commitment: tiny yes/no CTAs ("Comment 1 or 2", "Left or right?") → follow → later waitlist.
- Authority: show the source in small text ("USDA", "Study 2017, 49 trials") when a number is surprising.
- Social proof: only real ("most lifters do the left one" is fine as a framing; never fake numbers or fake comments).
- Liking: same mascot, voice, colours, sound logo every time.
- Scarcity: ONLY at app launch ("first 500 get Pro free") and only if true.

## 6. Content pillars — the 5 questions people ask (They Ask, You Answer)
1. **Problems** — "Why am I not losing fat?", "Why always hungry on a cut?"
2. **Comparisons** — X vs Y (our best format: food side-by-side, exercise wrong vs right, RDL vs deadlift)
3. **Cost** — "Is a coach worth it?", "Cheapest high-protein foods"
4. **Reviews** — honest takes on popular foods/trends (no supplement doses or brands)
5. **Best-of** — "4 best lifts for…", "best high-volume foods"
Topic source #1 = questions in OUR comments (see RUN.md "Comment loop"). #2 = PLAYBOOK topic bank. #3 = RESEARCH.md.

## 7. Daily-moment calendar (Contagious: triggers)
Mon: "new week" training · Tue: problem/answer · Wed: food comparison · Thu: training fix · Fri: "weekend fat-loss trap" · Sat: cost / best-of · Sun: meal prep / protein. Every day still has 3 reels (see RUN.md).

## 8. Jab / right hook (Vaynerchuk)
- NOW (audience mode): 100% jabs. No app, no links, no asks except comment/save/follow.
- AT LAUNCH (≈1,000 followers or app approved — Presko decides): 9 value posts : 1 ask (waitlist / app). Native per platform: TikTok raw + fast, IG clean + saveable, FB simple.

## 9. Visual identity (Public)
Black #050505, orange #FF6A1A, white; Anton headlines; series chip + episode #; F tile + FORGE wordmark; orange-muscle figure = mascot; 2.2 s end card + sound logo. Never other colours as main accents (no lime, no purple).

## 10. Growth system (Hacking Growth)
- **North Star:** new followers per week (all platforms).
- Leading metrics per video: 3-s hold, avg watch %, shares, saves, comments.
- **One test per week**, chosen Monday by ICE (Impact × Confidence × Ease, 1–10 each). Log hypothesis, result, keep/kill in WEEK.md.
- Growth loop: video → comments → best question becomes next "Answering your question" video → more comments.
